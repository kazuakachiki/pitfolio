1. Overview

Garage Log is an offline vehicle journal application designed to help users manage vehicle maintenance, modifications, vehicle setups, and track-day records.

2. Information We Collect

Garage Log does not require account registration and does not collect personal information through developer-operated servers. The application does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Vehicle information, maintenance records, parts records, setup information, and track-day records are stored locally on your device. The developer does not have access to these records.

4. Backup and Data Sharing

Garage Log provides user-initiated backup and restore features. If you export or share a backup file, its destination and handling depend on the storage location or third-party service you choose. Garage Log does not automatically upload your vehicle records to developer-operated servers.

5. Internet Connection

Garage Log's core functions work without an internet connection. The application does not require an account or cloud service to manage your records.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your records remain on your device until you edit or remove them, restore another backup, or uninstall the application. Backup copies exported outside the application must be managed and deleted separately.

7. Children's Privacy

Garage Log is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please visit the Garage Log Support page.
