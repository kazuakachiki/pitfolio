Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pitfolio is designed to work offline. Your vehicle records are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

1. Information You Enter

Pitfolio allows you to enter vehicle details, mileage, maintenance records, parts, modifications, setup values, track sessions, lap times, and related information. These records are stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection

Pitfolio does not require an account and does not collect personal information or vehicle records through a developer-operated server. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

3. Network Access

The core features of Pitfolio do not require an internet connection. The app does not automatically transmit your vehicle records to the developer.

4. Backup and Restore

Pitfolio provides user-initiated backup export and restore functionality. When you export a backup, you choose where to save or share it using the options provided by your device.

If you save a backup to a cloud storage provider or share it with another service, that provider's privacy practices and terms may apply.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Vehicle records remain on your device until you modify or delete them, restore different data, or remove the app and its local data. Deleting the app generally removes its locally stored data, subject to device backup and operating system behavior.

Exported backups are separate files and are not automatically deleted when you uninstall Pitfolio.

6. Third-Party Services

Pitfolio does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. Device operating system services may be used when you choose to export, save, share, or import files.

7. Children's Privacy

Pitfolio is not designed to collect information from children. No account registration or developer-operated data collection is required.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Pitfolio or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please visit Pitfolio Support.
