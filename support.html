Frequently Asked Questions

Does Garage Log require an account?

No. Garage Log works without account registration. Your vehicle records are stored locally on your device.

Can I use Garage Log offline?

Yes. Core features, including vehicle management, maintenance records, setups, and track records, work without an internet connection.

How do I back up my data?

Open Garage Log, go to Settings, and select the backup/export option. Save the exported file to a location you can access later.

How do I transfer data to a new device?

Export a backup from your old device. Transfer the backup file to your new device, then open Garage Log and use the restore/import option in Settings.

Does Garage Log automatically sync between devices?

No. Garage Log does not provide automatic cloud sync. Use backup and restore to transfer your records.

Can I record lap times?

Yes. You can manually record track sessions and lap times and compare compatible sessions.
