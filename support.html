Pitfolio Support

Find answers to common questions about Pitfolio, your offline vehicle journal.

Do I need an account?

No. Pitfolio does not require account registration or sign-in.

Does Pitfolio work offline?

Yes. Pitfolio's core features work without an internet connection.

Where is my data stored?

Your vehicle records are stored locally on your device, not on a developer-operated server.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings in Pitfolio and use the Backup option. Choose where to save the exported backup file.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings and select Restore. Choose a compatible Pitfolio backup file. Restoring data may replace your current local records, so export a backup first.

Does Pitfolio automatically record lap times?

No. Track sessions and lap times are entered manually. GPS tracking and external hardware are not required.

Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. You can add and manage multiple vehicles in your garage.

Does Pitfolio have a subscription?

No. Pitfolio is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

How can I change measurement units?

Pitfolio supports multiple measurement units for supported values, including distance, pressure, and temperature. Use the unit controls in the app.
